From Community to Catastrophe: Mapping Jewish Life and the Holocaust in Monor

A map of Jewish Monor reconstructs the institutions, homes and everyday spaces of a once-thriving community before the deportations of 1944. Places now encountered as ordinary streets are read as historical evidence.

The historical story

The Jewish community of Monor was established in 1862 by 25 families. Over subsequent decades its synagogue and institutions became part of the town’s economic, religious and social life.

During the Holocaust, anti-Jewish measures forced local Jews into designated housing and then to the brick factory, which served as a collection site for thousands from Monor and the surrounding region. Deportations to Auschwitz-Birkenau followed in July 1944.

The StoryMap connects individual streets and buildings to the community’s former presence, asking what a town’s present-day geography can still tell us about its past.
