Walking Memory: The Hero’s Path and the Construction of Warsaw Ghetto Memory

Lynne Feldman•StoryMap•Solo

The story

a walk thorugh the Hearo's path and questions of memory and its constrction

Where and when?

Wasrasw, Warsaw ghetto uprising and its memory

Central public-history question

how the memory of the Warsaw Ghetto has been constructed over time and by whom.

Why does this story matter?

Memory of an important historical event is constructed in many different ways—not only through physical monuments, but also through the ideas and choices behind them. This story matters because it encourages visitors to look beyond what is commemorated and, through guided questions, to think critically about how memory is constructed, who shapes it, and what may be absent.
