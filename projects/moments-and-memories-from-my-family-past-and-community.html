Testimony & Family Memory

Moments and Memories from my Family Past and Community

Eirini Anastasiadou•Short video•Solo

The story

The story of my grandfather, father and uncle working in public labour in 1949 leaving their home from Kilkis (Dipotamos) to Serres (Vrontou).

Where and when?

The motives and consequences for families, and the conditions of public labour one year after the Second World War and during the three-year Civil War in Greece, with a particular focus on Kilkis( Dipotamos- my previous family location) and Serres (Vrontou- during the 3-year Civil War).

Central public-history question

The story provides insight into the lives of ordinary people and workers who struggled to make a living, offering a personal perspective on the social and economic conditions that shaped their lives and experiences in a particular time and place. At the same time, it also highlights the joyful side of everyday life, showing how people found happiness in simple moments, shared experiences, and their attempts to have fun despite the difficulties they faced.

Why does this story matter?

Because the story is closely connected to my community and its local history, it provides a personal connection to the past and helps preserve the memories and experiences of the people who lived there during a difficult historically time period.

Sources & evidence

Family Photographs that are situated in my personal and family archive in the General Archives of Kilkis. And an interview from my aunt taken place in her home on the 25th September 2026.

Public-history lens

This project is presented within Testimony, Family Memory & Community Archives. Read it not only for what happened in the past, but for how sources, memory, place, visibility and interpretation shape what becomes public history.
