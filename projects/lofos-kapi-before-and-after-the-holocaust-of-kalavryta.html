Place & Local Memory

Lofos Kapi- Before and After the Holocaust of Kalavryta

ELENI GKOUVELOU- PANAGIOTA KATSARAMENIDOU•Photo essay•Team

The story

A memorial monument, which was built at the same place where the massacre took place, up to the hill is not only a monument but also a reminder to the memory for all the people who resist. The local events every year, at the 13th of December, is not only a tribute but the living memory.

Where and when?

Kalavryta, Achaea, Greece from 13th of December 1943. The story situated in Kalavryta a greek small city in the prefecture of Achaia. The main historical fact is the massacre in 13th of December 1943. It concern more the local community how this fact and especially the memorial monument reminds at the local community the past and why people around the world visit the memorial monument. The story will be digital.

Central public-history question

How a messacre has change the local memory and the landscape?

Why does this story matter?

A memorial monument is a reminder for human rights violation and all people should be against to these energies. Also it's a kind of honor for the people who have been killed.
