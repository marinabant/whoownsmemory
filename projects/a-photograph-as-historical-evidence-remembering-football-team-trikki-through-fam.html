A Photograph as Historical Evidence: Remembering Football Team ''Trikki'' Through Family and Community

The story

One photograph of A.S. Trikki from the 1950s combines different archival sources. Rather than telling the complete history of Trikki, it will use one image to explore how soccer was remembered within one local community.and asks what it can reveal about the people and community behind the soccer team.

Where and when?

The story is situated in Trikala, Greece, and focuses primarily on the history of A.S. Trikki and its local soccer community during the 1950s, following the club's establishment in 1949. It concerns the soccer players, their families, supporters, and the wider local community that preserved and transmitted memories of the team. The project connects a family-held photograph from the period with a later newspaper publication that revisited the history of A.S. Trikki in 2009.

Central public-history question

How can a single photograph of A.S. Trikki be used to reconstruct the personal and community memories associated with soccer in 1950s Trikala?

Why does this story matter?

The history of a soccer team is not just about results and players. Through these photographs, we see the city’s development, social relationships, a sense of community, the residents’ connection to sports, and local pride.This story matters because photographs do more than preserve images of the past: they can become historical evidence through the memories and stories attached to them. The photograph of A.S. Trikki preserves a particular moment in the history of a local football community, while the later newspaper article demonstrates how that history was remembered and brought into the public sphere. By bringing together family memory, photographic evidence, and community history, the project explores how local histories are preserved across generations. It also asks how an ordinary photograph can provide a point of entry into a larger history of belonging, identity, and community life.
