Talk to the Past

An interactive rotary telephone introduces the memories of Mieczysław Gnojek, deported from Poland to Germany for forced labour in 1943 at the age of fifteen. The interface asks what was lost when childhood and youth were interrupted.

This is an original visual from the participant’s submitted work. It provides a point of entry to the project’s historical argument and digital form.

Chapter 01

Calling a remembered past

The rotary-telephone interface connects a contemporary visitor to records of Mieczysław Gnojek, deported from Poland to Nazi Germany for forced labour in 1943 while still a teenager.

Chapter 02

Young people and forced labour

Memoirs published decades after the events illuminate the vulnerability of young forced labourers and the way coerced work interrupted ordinary youth.

Chapter 03

An intentionally broken line

The project uses the metaphor of a telephone call to question the distance between a past that cannot be recovered directly and the memories people read and interpret today.
