Place & Local Memory

Modern Salamis and the Memory of Darius' Ghost

Antonis Chaliakopoulos•Digital exhibition / web story•Solo

The story

A story about the island of Salamis and how its people relate to its classical past.

Where and when?

Digital exhibition. The story is about contemporary Salamis with photographs taken between 2024 and 2026.

Central public-history question

How do the people of Salamis relate to the classical past and the memory of the battle of Salamis?

Why does this story matter?

It facilitates an undestanding of how people relate to the past.

Sources & evidence

- Personal photographic archive. - Bibiliography related to the battle of Salamis and its reception.

Public-history lens

This project is presented within Place, Local Memory, Monuments & Heritage. Read it not only for what happened in the past, but for how sources, memory, place, visibility and interpretation shape what becomes public history.

Explore the project

The exhibition prioritises direct access to submitted material. Locally supplied files are preserved in this website; interactive works are embedded alongside them where available.
