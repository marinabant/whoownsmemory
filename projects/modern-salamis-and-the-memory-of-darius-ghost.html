Central public-history question
How do people living on Salamis relate to the island’s classical past?
Chapter 01

A classical past in a living landscape

The Greek island of Salamis is closely associated with the naval battle of 480 BCE. The project asks how people living there today inhabit and interpret that reputation.

Chapter 02

What the present can tell us

Contemporary photographs and digital storytelling place local environments and everyday observation beside accounts of the ancient past.

Chapter 03

Who represents the island?

By juxtaposing classical narratives and modern viewpoints, the work considers how public histories acquire authority and what alternative local memories become visible.
