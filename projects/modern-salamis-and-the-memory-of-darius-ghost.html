The historical story

Question of memory

How do people living on Salamis relate to the island’s classical past?

The ancient Battle of Salamis occupies a prominent place in narratives of Greek antiquity. On the island today, however, the past is encountered in monuments, familiar landscapes, public discourse and everyday movement.

Antonis Chaliakopoulos uses a contemporary photographic approach to examine how residents relate to classical heritage rather than treating antiquity as a self-evident background.

The interactive web story invites visitors to examine the distance between the celebrated ancient battle and the island’s present-day public memory.
