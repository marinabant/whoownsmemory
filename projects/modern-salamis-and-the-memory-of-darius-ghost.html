Modern Salamis and the Memory of Darius' Ghost

A digital exhibition exploring how people living on the island of Salamis relate to its classical past, especially the memory and reception of the Battle of Salamis. The project draws on photographs taken between 2024 and 2026.

About the project

A digital exhibition exploring how people living on the island of Salamis relate to its classical past, especially the memory and reception of the Battle of Salamis. The project draws on photographs taken between 2024 and 2026.

Historical setting

Digital exhibition. The story is about contemporary Salamis with photographs taken between 2024 and 2026.

Why this story matters

It facilitates an understanding of how people relate to the past.

The guiding question

How do the people of Salamis relate to the classical past and the memory of the battle of Salamis?
