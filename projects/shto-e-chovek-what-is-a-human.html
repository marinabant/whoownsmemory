Digital Exhibition · First Cycle 2026 · Testimony & Family Memory

“Що е човек?” — “What Is a Human?”

A visual history of one family story from Senokos, near the Serbia–Bulgaria border, places testimony, drawings and landscape together to ask what it means to live with poverty, movement and historical change.

The work in images

02

The historical story

Kremena Dimitrova draws on an ethnographic account of Deda Pepu and life in Senokos, a mountain village in Serbia near the Bulgarian border.

The graphic work combines testimony, historical images, landscape photographs and documentary material to connect one life with poverty, cross-border movement and patterns of marginalisation.

Its comic-like visual language allows personal memory and regional context to sit side by side, leaving room for the subject’s own account.

03

Places, memory and interpretation

Eastern Europe and the Balkans, the Bulgaria-Serbia border, village Senokos (Serbia) 20th Century Bulgarian-Serbian community. Digital and printed to be shared online, website, social media, and also printed and posted to the person behind the story. The story is intended to be used as an educational, accessible, shareable, creative/visual.

Public-history question

What can one life tell us about the memory of poverty and migration across generations?
