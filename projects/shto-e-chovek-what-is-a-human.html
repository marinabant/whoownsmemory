The story

When change is NOT the only constant: poverty: how one’s lived experiences/historical memory of the 20th C are still the reality in the 21st Century in the Western Balkans geographical region. Visibilise emotional memories: the story speaks to poverty and the difficult life in the Western Balkan mountains, migration… It brings attention to an otherwise “forgotten” part of Eastern Europe and the Western Balkans and the Bulgaria-Serbia border, village Senokos (Serbia) and one of the poorest European regions. The story exemplifies how life in the mountain is characterised by severe economic hardship and isolation due to extreme geography and a lack of basic infrastructure. There are only several people still living in the village today and this is the story of one of them in his own words. The story also connects to migration, the “Balkan Route”, and the plight of migrants crossing the border.

Where and when?

Eastern Europe and the Balkans, the Bulgaria-Serbia border, village Senokos (Serbia) 20th Century Bulgarian-Serbian community. Digital and printed to be shared online, website, social media, and also printed and posted to the person behind the story. The story is intended to be used as an educational, accessible, shareable, creative/visual resource (in addition to the report I have already produced as part of the project: (Aug 2024 - Sep 2025) Endangered Wooden Architecture Programme (EWAP) Cultural Grant (Arcadia), Hosted by Oxford Brookes University and Delivered in Collaboration with CyArk - Vernacular Housing Compounds along the Borderline of Bulgaria and Serbia in the Western Balkan Mountain - Documenting Endangered Wooden Buildings and Related Oral Stories: https://ewap.brookes.ac.uk/report/5e652a98-d929-4a14-a8c0-ae5693a0eb20) Dimitrova, K (2025). Ethnographic report, Serbian villages, 2025 | EWAP3121SG-PPA-0002. Vernacular Architecture of the Western Balkans (Bulgaria/Serbia) collection.
