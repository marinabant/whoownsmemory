Why this story matters

It brings attention to an otherwise “forgotten” part of Eastern Europe and the Western Balkans and the Bulgaria-Serbia border, village Senokos (Serbia) and one of the poorest European regions. The story exemplifies how life in the mountain is characterised by severe economic hardship and isolation due to extreme geography and a lack of basic infrastructure. There are only several people still living in the village today and this is the story of one of them in his own words. The story also connects to migration, the “Balkan Route”, and the plight of migrants crossing the border.
