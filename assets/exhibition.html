Digital Exhibition · 2026

Many stories. Many voices.
One shared future.

Final project submissions bring together local history, family memory, archival traces, difficult pasts, migration, place, testimony, digital media and questions of democratic access to history.

The exhibition brings the first cycle of Who Owns Memory? into public space. Project files are locally preserved in this website wherever publication-ready material was supplied; interactive projects are embedded directly in their project pages where technically possible.
